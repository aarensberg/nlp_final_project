Baseline: TF-IDF + logistic regression

What it is

  • TF-IDF vectors over unigrams and bigrams
  • Logistic regression over the 100 types
  • ≈ 4 min of training on CPU

Why it is hard to beat

On LEDGAR, a clause's type often hinges on a few keywords: “governing law”, “counterparts”, “severability”. A lexical model captures them very well.

Permitted and prohibited uses

Intended use

  • Help with triaging SEC-style English clauses
  • Systematic human review
  • Clauses below the threshold → lawyer
  • Precedent search as a starting point

Do not use for

  • Deciding a clause's type or scope alone
  • European law, French, German: not validated
  • Exhaustively detecting risks or unfair clauses
  • Missing types: GDPR, force majeure, limitation of liability…
  • Finding a specific notice period: non-semantic filter
  • Drawing fairness conclusions about people: proxies
Classifying a clause type is not judging its legal validity.

Limitations and next steps

Measured limitations

  • BERT alone below TF-IDF; ensemble ≈ +0.7 pt
  • Near-synonymous labels: Applicable Laws / Governing Laws, Waivers / No Waivers
  • Lease clauses and rare classes
  • Retrieval: the notion of notice period is not understood
  • One seed, one machine, beta Python

Proposed next steps (not done)

  • Controlled experiment: 256 vs 512 tokens, 2 vs more epochs with early stopping, several seeds
  • Annotated European clauses, missing types
  • Structured extraction of deadlines (role + duration + day type)
  • Merging of near-synonyms validated by lawyers
